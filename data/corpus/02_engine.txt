BoundedGlitchEngine, or BGE, is a small trainable chatbot. It needs no machine learning libraries. It runs on Flask and plain Python, so it works on a phone in Termux.

BGE answers in three layers. First it checks taught answers. Then it searches the corpus. If both fail, it falls back to a Markov chain, or admits it does not know.

Taught answers win over everything. You teach the bot with the command teach, followed by a question, an equals sign, and an answer. Taught answers are saved to knowledge.json and survive restarts.

Corpus search uses TF-IDF similarity. Every passage in the corpus folder is indexed. Rare words carry more weight than common words. The passage closest to your question is returned.

The Markov fallback is a word chain trained on the corpus and on taught answers. It babbles from words related to your question. It is rough, and it improves with more text.

To train the bot, place text files in the data corpus folder and type train. Training reloads every file and rebuilds the index and the chain. No restart is needed.

The forget command removes a taught question. The stats command shows how many questions, passages, words, and Markov states the bot holds. The help command lists the commands.

More text means better answers. Write clear passages, separate them with blank lines, and keep each one focused on a single idea.
